A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

What is Storestrike?

Storestrike is the proposed commercial identity for the commerce command center: a shared workspace for AI-assisted strategy, campaign planning, creative briefs, and analysis of information you provide.

Does it connect to my store and ad accounts?

The current build uses information you supply. Live store, advertising, payment, and media-generation connections are not yet configured. The interactive preview uses sample content.

Will the crew publish ads or spend money?

The current product helps prepare plans and drafts for your review. It does not automatically publish campaigns, spend ad budgets, or run scheduled automations.

What does the annual plan save?

Twelve monthly payments total $1,164. The proposed annual plan is $997 upfront, saving $167 per year, about 14.3%. Its equivalent monthly cost is $83.08.

Can I subscribe today?

Not yet. This page previews the commercial offer. The current application runs locally and uses an installed, signed-in Codex account. Hosted onboarding, subscription billing, and final usage terms still need to be completed.
